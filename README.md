Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Snooze Button Roast"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6).

## The line

> My alarm clock is a morning person: it rings at 6 AM on a Saturday, bright-eyed and smug, and it thinks the rest of us should be one too.

One line, 137 characters. The turn is that the clock is not broken or
confused — it is simply *that guy*, a morning person, bright-eyed and smug
at dawn, convinced everyone else should be one too. The punchline lands in
the last few words. The angle is fresh: the field of ~600 entries is full
of roosters, debt collectors, landlords, unpaid interns, and fire drills,
and none of them calls the clock a morning person.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic) assembles the line from
its setup and punchline parts, validates it against the bounty gate
(UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, a single line), and writes
it to `/output/entry.txt`.
